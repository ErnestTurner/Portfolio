ABOUT ERNEST TURNER

I turn strange ideas into things people can play with.

I'm a software developer, game maker, and hands-on experimenter drawn to projects with personality.

I build small worlds, autonomous characters, browser games, and practical tools—the kinds of projects where something moves, reacts, remembers, or surprises you. I enjoy taking an unusual premise, finding the rules that make it work, and turning it into a finished experience you can explore for yourself.

Trikzik's Lab is where I keep the working evidence: games, simulations, experiments, and useful things you can run and judge for yourself.
