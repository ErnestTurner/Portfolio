Pocket Mote privacy policy

Effective September 28, 2026 · Trikzik Labs

No data collection

Pocket Mote runs offline. It has no network permission, advertising, analytics, accounts, or third-party tracking. It does not collect or share personal data.

The app does not read charging or battery state, motion, ambient light, other apps, notifications, the microphone, camera, location, contacts, or device identifiers.

Information stored on your device

Touches affect the local digital habitat. Pocket Mote stores the pet's identity and progress, web and home state, interactions, settings, and local history on the device so play can continue later. This information is not sent to Trikzik Labs or any third party. Save timestamps are used locally to update bounded elapsed age when the app returns. The app does not simulate activity in the background.

Sound

Optional sounds play only while Pocket Mote is open. No audio is recorded.

Backup and restore

Optional backup and restore uses Android's system file picker. Pocket Mote reads or writes only the file selected by the user. If the user selects a cloud-storage provider, that provider handles the exported file under its own privacy terms. Restoring a backup requires confirmation before it replaces the current pet.

Deleting information

Clearing Pocket Mote's app storage or uninstalling the app deletes its local data. Exported backups remain where the user saved them and can be deleted separately.
