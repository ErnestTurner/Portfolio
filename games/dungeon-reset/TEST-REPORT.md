- JavaScript syntax checked for `core.js` and `game.js`.
- Static files served over local HTTP rather than restricted `file://` URLs.
- `dist/build-manifest.json` records a SHA-256 digest for every shipped file.

- The packaged local release is served and smoke-tested independently after archive creation.

## Tested behavior outside automated assertions

- Original generated room and atlas artwork were visually inspected at full resolution and in the title and repair screens.
- The first visible browser handoff showed the complete game-native title screen rather than a starter page.
- The task layout was inspected at desktop and 390-pixel mobile widths. At narrow width the room precedes a two-column clipboard; landscape or a larger display remains the intended touch experience.
- The read-only WebMCP inspection tool registered, returned live game state, and intentionally rejected an invalid input object without changing game state.

## Known limitations

- Keyboard and mouse are the primary tested controls. Touch was tested through Chrome mobile emulation, not on physical iOS or Android hardware.
- Browser fullscreen support varies. The game remains playable without fullscreen.
- Scores are specific to the browser origin and disappear if the player clears storage. A blocked storage write is reported on the results screen.
- Runs in progress are intentionally not saved. There is no controller support or remapping.
- The animated battle has textual status and scoring but no full screen-reader narration.
